# Trademarks and branding

This document is an **engineering checklist**. It is **not** a trademark clearance opinion.

## Independence

FasterTest is an independent open-source project. It is **not** affiliated with, endorsed by, or
sponsored by Grafana Labs. It is **not** an official Grafana or k6 product.

Grafana, k6, and related marks are trademarks of Grafana Labs or their respective owners.

## Product wording

Prefer factual wording such as:

- “Open-source load and stress testing platform powered by the k6 engine”

Avoid wording that implies FasterTest is an official edition, distribution, partnership, or
replacement of k6/Grafana.

## Name risk (manual legal review)

Do **not** assume the name “FasterTest” is legally clear. Before commercial launch, SaaS
monetization, company registration, or paid marketing, recommend trademark searches in relevant
classes (software / performance testing), including:

- WIPO Global Brand Database
- USPTO
- EUIPO
- Relevant national databases
- Confusingly similar marks (e.g. FASTTEST, FASTER TEST, FAST TEST)

**Do not rename the project in this repository without explicit owner approval.**
